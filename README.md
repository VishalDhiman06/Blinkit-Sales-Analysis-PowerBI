# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Analysis

### Interactive Power BI Dashboard to Uncover Retail Sales Insights

</div>

---

## 📝 Description & Purpose

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes **sales performance, customer satisfaction and inventory distribution** across outlets, turning raw retail data into clear business insights.

**Problem statement:** Which products, outlet types and locations drive the most sales, and how can a retail business use data to better understand its performance and find opportunities for improvement?

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- 🟨 **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**
- 🧹 **Power Query:** data cleaning and transformation
- 🧱 **Data modeling**
- 🧮 **DAX:** KPI measures
- 🎨 **Dashboard design and data storytelling**

---

## 📂 Data Source

Blinkit-themed retail grocery sales dataset, loaded into Power BI and cleaned in Power Query. It covers **8,523 item records** with these fields:

- 🛍️ **Item details:** item type, fat content, item visibility
- 🏪 **Outlet details:** outlet type, outlet size, location tier, establishment year
- 💰 **Performance:** sales and customer rating

- 🎚️ **Slicers:** Outlet Location Type, Outlet Size, Item Type
- 🔄 **Metric switch:** Total Sales, Avg Sales, Number of Items, Avg Rating
- ♻️ **One-click reset** button to clear all filters (custom icon)
- 🧭 **Navigation icons** and a clean, Blinkit-inspired yellow and green theme

### 💡 Key insights

| 🔍 Area | 📌 Finding |
|---|---|
| 🥛 **Fat Content** | Regular fat drives about **65%** of sales ($776K) vs about 35% for low fat ($425K) |
| 🥬 **Item Type** | **Fruits & Vegetables** and **Snack Foods** lead at $0.18M each |
| 🏪 **Outlet Type** | **Supermarket Type 1** generates about **65%** of revenue ($787.6K) |
| 📏 **Outlet Size** | **Medium** outlets lead with about 42%, then Small (37%) and High (21%) |
| 📍 **Location** | **Tier 3** is the top market ($472K), ahead of Tier 2 ($393K) and Tier 1 ($336K) |
| 📅 **Establishment Year** | Steady at about $130K per year with one peak of about $205K |
| ⚖️ **Ratings & Avg Sales** | Nearly the same across outlet types, so growth depends on **item volume** |

<details>
<summary><b>👉 Click to see weak spots and recommendations</b></summary>

<br>

- **Weakest categories:** Seafood ($0.01M), Breakfast and Starchy Foods ($0.02M each). These may need better promotion or stock planning.
- **Underperforming outlet types:** Grocery Store and Supermarket Type 2 and 3 sell far fewer items than Type 1.
- **Opportunity:** Since ratings and prices are consistent, increasing item volume in weaker outlets and categories is the clearest growth path.

</details>
